# Daily Wage Workers Payment

This contract template governs the payment obligations for daily wage labor arrangements, providing automated calculation of compensation owed to workers based on days worked and overtime hours rendered.

## Purpose

This template establishes a clear framework for calculating remuneration in daily wage employment relationships, ensuring transparency and accuracy in payment determinations. It supports both standard daily rate compensation and premium overtime rate calculations.

## Parties

The contract recognizes two parties:

- **Employer**: The party engaging the worker and obligated to provide compensation
- **Worker**: The party providing labor services and entitled to receive payment

## Payment Structure

The template calculates total compensation based on the following components:

### Base Compensation
Daily wages are computed by multiplying the number of days worked by the agreed-upon daily rate, as specified in the contract terms.

### Overtime Compensation  
Additional compensation for overtime hours is calculated by multiplying overtime hours worked by the predetermined overtime rate, which is typically set at a premium above the standard daily rate.

### Total Payment Calculation
The total amount owed shall be the sum of base compensation plus overtime compensation, rendered in the currency specified in the contract.

## Usage Example

**Given the following contract terms:**
- Daily Rate: 150.00 USD per day
- Overtime Rate: 25.00 USD per hour

**For a work period with:**
- Days Worked: 5 days
- Overtime Hours: 4 hours

**The calculation yields:**
- Base Pay: 5 days × 150.00 USD = 750.00 USD
- Overtime Pay: 4 hours × 25.00 USD = 100.00 USD
- **Total Payment Due: 850.00 USD**

## Technical Implementation

This template conforms to the Accord Project Template Specification and can be executed by the Cicero engine for automated contract processing and payment calculation.
